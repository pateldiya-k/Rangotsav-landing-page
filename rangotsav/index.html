Design notes

Why it looks this way

Intended users

Families, college groups and neighbours looking for a safe, fun festival day, mostly browsing on phones and sharing the link in group chats.

Style choices

  • Four festival colours (pink, saffron, teal, violet) on a warm cream base.
  • Yatra One headings have a festive Indian feel; Poppins keeps text easy to read.
  • Variables control all colours and fonts, with automatic dark mode.

Reusable components

Button (primary and ghost), Section header, and Card with a colour accent are reused for activities, schedule and passes.
